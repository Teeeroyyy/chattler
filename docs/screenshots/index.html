A chat you can work in

Badges, emotes, replies, first-time chatters, channel point redemptions and moderation buttons on every line.

Everything in one window

Mod actions, Subs and Highlights open as panels beside the chat - or in windows of their own, on another screen.

Several chats at once

Split a tab as many ways as you like, and keep as many tabs as you need.

Who you are talking to

Click any name. Moderators get a second tab holding everything the team has done to that person.

AutoMod and suspicious users

Held messages and flagged people come to you, in the chat you are already reading.

Polls and predictions

Start one in two clicks, watch it fill, lock it and pay it out - without leaving the chat.

Whispers, without leaving

Read and answer private messages in a panel beside your chats.

All the emotes

Twitch, 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ, in chat and in the picker.

Settings for everything

Each heading opens on its own, so the page stays short. Everything starts switched off - you turn on what you want.

Eight themes

The same chat, four of them. Every theme is a plain text file, so you can change one or write your own.
